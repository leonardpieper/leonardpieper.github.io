Instructions

The rulesin a few minutes

Place paintings on your timeline, guess who made them, and be first to fill it.

Try a round first →

Setup

  1. 1

    Shuffle all cards together to form the draw pile.

  2. 2

    Deal one card to each player or team, showing the information side (Movement, Year, Artist and Name).

  3. 3

    Place the draw pile within reach of all players.

Goal

Be the first player or team to reach the target number of cards in your timeline.

How to play

On your turn

  1. 1

    Draw a card from the top of the pile, keeping the image side face up so everyone can see the cropped artwork.

  2. 2

    Place the card on your timeline in the correct position.

  3. 3

    Guess the artist and title of the artwork.

  4. 4

    Reveal the back of the card to check your guess.

Placed correctly? The card stays in your timeline.

Wrong position? The card is discarded and your turn ends.

Artist and title correct? You may move one card from the top of the draw pile to the bottom. Use it to make the next draw easier or harder for the next player.

Additional rules

Stealing

If a non-active player already has a card of the same painting somewhere in their timeline, they may attempt to steal the card on top of the draw pile at any moment.

  • To steal, call out the steal and name which of your own paintings you believe the top card depicts.
  • Correct: you take the drawn card into your own timeline.
  • Incorrect: you must discard all cards with the painting you called.
  • If more than one player tries to steal the same card, whoever calls first gets the guess.
  • After a successful steal the active player draws again, taking another turn immediately with a fresh draw.

FAQ
